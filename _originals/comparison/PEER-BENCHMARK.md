# Comparison: Aldeas Infantiles SOS Paraguay vs Peer Organizations

> **Purpose**: Benchmark SOS Paraguay against (a) international peers in child welfare / sponsorship, and (b) local Paraguayan ONGs to find gaps and inspiration.
> **Method**: Public revenue reports, program pages, and one financial-data page from SOS International's own transparency portal.
> **Last updated**: 2026-08-21

---

## A. International peers — child sponsorship / residential care / protection

### A1. SOS Children's Villages INTERNATIONAL (the umbrella)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| 2024 combined revenue | **€1.72 billion** (~USD 1.86B) |
| Donations by individuals | €782M (45%) |
| Government subsidies (domestic programs) | €608M (35%) |
| Total expenditure | €1.65B |
| % to programs | 76% |
| % to fund development | 13% |
| % to overhead | 11% |
| Audited? | Yes, annually by independent auditors |
| Public executive salaries | Disclosed (President/CEO/CPO/COO/Special Rep = €180k–220k gross in 2019) |
| Transparency URL | https://www.sos-childrensvillages.org/financial-data |

**Key takeaway for SOS Paraguay**: the umbrella *has* public financial data — but the Paraguay national association does NOT publish its own. They could/should mirror this transparency.

### A2. SOS Children's Villages ESPAÑA (2024)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Children/youth served in 2024 | **59,317** (10% YoY growth) |
| Families served | 8,471 |
| Spain domestic programs | 29,759 beneficiaries |
| International programs | 29,558 beneficiaries |
| Spain programs: 32 Day Centers, 9 Family Programs, 5 Early Childhood Education Centers | active network |
| President (Pedro Puig) publicly advocates on child poverty rates | strong public profile |

**Comparison vs Paraguay**:
- Spain serves 60× more beneficiaries than Paraguay (59,317 vs ~1,000)
- Spain has a documented **Memory 2024** (annual report) download — Paraguay doesn't
- Spain president actively appears in press — Paraguay's director is invisible in public
- This is **the most directly comparable benchmark** since Paraguay is also a national member of the federation

### A3. Compassion International

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| HQ | Colorado Springs, USA |
| Model | **Sponsorship** at $43/month, tied to specific child |
| Letters processed | ~9M/year between sponsors and children (2019 data) |
| Sponsor portal | Centralized communication center (login-based) |
| Email list | 950,000+ subscribers |
| Public financial integrity page | Yes (Charity Navigator rated) |
| Tech | Full SPA, login portals for sponsors |

**Key insight**: This is the *exact* product Aldea SOS Paraguay is missing. Compassion is a sponsorship machine — letters, photos, portal login, dashboards. SOS Paraguay could launch a similar model for PY sponsorships.

### A4. Plan International (formerly Foster Parents Plan)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | 1937, "Foster Parents Plan for War Children" |
| Model | Child sponsorship + programs |
| Donor loyalty team | Dedicated 800 number + donor portal |
| Active sponsor visit program | In-person visits to sponsored child |
| Tech | Online child-selection portal |

**Key insight**: Plan has a donor-visit experience. Paraguay's tourism + diaspora market could power a "visit your sponsored child" program — but this needs safeguarding around child protection.

### A5. ChildFund International

- Public annual reports + 990s downloadable
- "International offices" model (autonomous + federation)
- Strong digital transparency
- Has an explicit "We don't use your gift for administrative costs" framing

### A6. World Vision

- 35.2M people assisted globally (2024) — including 19M children
- 87 natural disasters responded to
- Massive scale
- Active digital storytelling

### A7. War Child Alliance

- 2024 revenue exceeded budget by 12%
- 100% growth in children reached (after merger consolidation)
- **Multi-entity federation** (like SOS) — annual report as a federation

### A8. Save the Children

- Operates in 120+ countries
- Members in 29 countries
- Public financials + Charity Navigator rating
- Strong digital storytelling + emergency campaigns

### A9. Terre des Hommes

- 82% donations to projects in 2025
- Innovation focus (environmental rights, online exploitation, forced labor)
- Annual reports downloadable

## B. Paraguayan ONGs (local peers)

### B1. Fundación Paraguaya (Martin Burt)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Founded | 1985 (during Stroessner era — act of courage) |
| HQ | Asunción |
| Model | Social enterprise, micro-finance pioneer |
| Programs | Poverty solutions via entrepreneurship |
| Notable | Self-sufficient "San Francisco" farm; teaches poverty graduation methodology globally |

**Different from SOS**: Not child-welfare, but youth economic empowerment. They are the gold-standard Paraguayan NGO brand for innovative anti-poverty models. SOS could partner on transition-to-adulthood programs for Aldea youth.

### B2. CIRD (Centro de Información y Recursos para el Desarrollo)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Revenue | $20.8M (Aug 2024 zoominfo data) |
| Programs | Training, immunization advocacy (currently using WhatsApp + RVE to send 25K+ messages to parents for child immunization) |
| Tech | **Actually using WhatsApp at scale** for child welfare — direct model SOS could adopt |

**Insight**: CIRD's WhatsApp-based child immunization model is exactly the kind of digital low-cost high-impact intervention that SOS Paraguay could replicate for family-strengthening communication.

### B3. UNICEF Paraguay

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Model | UN agency, country office |
| 2024 Annual Report | Public PDF download available |
| Programs | Country Programme 2020-2024 evaluated, transitioning to next cycle |
| Tech | Open data, open.unicef.org |

### B4. Fundación Solidaridad

### B5. Fundación Cimientos

### B6. Fundación Alda

### B7. TECHO Paraguay

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

## E. Specific competitive gaps for SOS Paraguay

1. **No online donation** — every single peer above has it. This is the #1 unlock.
2. **No annual report download** — even TECHO Paraguay publishes one.
3. **No sponsor portal** — even Aldeas Infantiles SOS Spain has it via the federation.
4. **No charity rating** — Charity Navigator, Give.org, etc.
5. **No Spanish/English version** — even if limited to "donate from abroad" page.
6. **No TikTok** — every major peer is there.
7. **No Google Ad Grants** — free $10k/mo available to any qualified nonprofit.
8. **No volunteer portal** — separate from "trabaja con nosotros" — for non-paid volunteers.
