C. Corporate partnerships

C1. Expand "Regalamos Sonrisas" to 3+ retail partners

Qué: Replicate TUPI partnership with Areté, Personal, Tigo, Stock, Casa Rica

Evidence: TUPI partnership ran 6 years — proven. Areté is already a supermarket ally.

Market size: Each retail partner could contribute USD 30-100k/yr

Plan: Replicate contract template; approach Tigo (large) and Personal (already mentioned in old posts)

Revenue potential: Gs. 100-300M/yr per partner × 2-3 = Gs. 300-900M

Safeguarding: Strong

C2. Matched giving

Qué: When an employee of partner corp donates, the corp matches

Evidence: Standard in US via Benevity; puede ser replicated in PY

Plan: Pilot with one big ally (Itaú); expand

C3. Sponsor-an-event corporate packages

Qué: Tiered packages (Gs. 5M Bronze, Gs. 20M Gold) for "Amigos SOS Annual Gala"

Evidence: TECHO Paraguay runs annual galas; Teletón runs Comilona

Revenue potential: Gs. 50-200M/yr

C4. B2B licensing of brand/trademark

Qué: License SOS brand to products (limited)

Evidence: Pink Ribbon, WWF Panda, (RED) all do this

Risks: Brand dilution — be careful

C5. Corporate volunteer days (priced packages)

Qué: Sell "team volunteer day at Aldea" packages to corporations (Gs. 5-15M per day)

Evidence: TECHO does volunteer days; Habitat for Humanity; many NGOs

Market size: All 18+ allies

Plan: Pilot with Ueno Bank

Revenue potential: Gs. 50-150M/yr

Safeguarding: HIGH RISK — children + corporate volunteers need careful protocol

C6. Skills-based volunteering (pro bono)

Qué: Recruit pro bono legal/tech/marketing from aliados corporativos

Evidence: Taproot Foundation model

Revenue potential: Equivalent to Gs. 100-500M in services
