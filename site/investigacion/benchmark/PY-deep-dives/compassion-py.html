Compassion International — PY Deep Dive

Por qué importa: Compassion is the most directly relevant peer. They run an identical model (sponsorship + child development) but with vastly superior digital infrastructure.

At a glance
HQ Colorado Springs, USA
Model Sponsorship ($43/mo por child)
Children served 2M+ worldwide
Letters processed ~9M/year between sponsors and children (2019)
Sponsor portal Login-based, dashboard, photo trimestral, letter exchange
Email list 950,000+ subscribers
Charity Navigator 4-star rated
Tech Full SPA, login portals, mobile-responsive

Qué they do that SOS PY doesn't

  1. Sponsorship portal: each sponsor has a login, sees their sponsored child, exchanges letters, gets trimestral photos
  2. Specific child ↔ specific donor model — high emotional investment
  3. Quarterly photo + report of sponsored child
  4. Donor loyalty team with dedicated phone line
  5. Online donation with card + recurring
  6. Multilingual site (10+ languages)
  7. Active redes sociales ad spend

Qué SOS PY could adapt (without copying)

Insight Adaptation
Sponsorship portal "Apadrina una Aldea" — house-level, not child-level (better safeguarding)
Letter exchange Adult-letter exchange model — between padrino and aldea house (with casa staff)
Quarterly photo Quarterly "house update" with anonymized photos
Donor loyalty team Zunilda Baruja is already this — formalize the role
Charity Navigator rating Apply for Fundación Lealtad (Spain) or Charity Navigator equivalents

Qué we no know about Compassion PY específicamente

  • Country-level PY revenue (Compassion started PY operations in 2001)
  • PY-specific digital infrastructure
  • Local partnerships
