# Stream E. Earned income / social enterprise

## E. Earned income / social enterprise

### E1. Bakery / catering kitchen (model: Maggie's Centres, Cookie Cart)
**Qué**: Train Aldea youth in bakery, run a social-enterprise bakery
**Evidence**: Many NGOs run bakeries; Casa de la Esperanza (AR), Hogar Bambi
**Market size**: PY bakery market USD 500M
**Plan**: Pilot in Aldea Luque; sell to local hotels + catering for events
**Revenue potential**: Gs. 50-300M/yr
**Safeguarding**: Strong (with proper labor safeguards for minors)

### E2. Aldea experience touristic visits
**Qué**: Paid visits to the aldeas (educational tours, "voluntourism")
**Evidence**: Common in LatAm (Peru, Bolivia NGOs)
**Risks**: HIGH safeguarding risk — children interacting with visitors
**Plan**: ONLY if safeguarding protocol strict; consider "behind-the-scenes" tour at HQ

### E3. Merchandise store (t-shirts, mugs, bags)
**Qué**: Branded merchandise — SOS logo products
**Evidence**: Every major NGO has merchandise
**Plan**: Online store via Shopify + Bancard

### E4. Artisan products by Aldea youth
**Qué**: Youth-produced crafts sold online
**Evidence**: Starfish Project, Purpose Jewelry models
**Plan**: Etsy store + local markets

### E5. Online courses / workshops (NGO management, child protection)
**Qué**: SOS federation expertise → paid courses for other NGOs
**Plan**: Train-the-trainer online course

### E6. "Sponsor-a-tree" agroforestry
**Qué**: Plant trees in aldeas; carbon credits + sponsor
**Evidence**: Common model in Latin America
**Plan**: Pilot in San Ignacio Misiones
