## Puntuación

Each stream scored on 4 axes (1-5, higher = better):
- **Speed** — how rápido to primero PYG in
- **Fit** — asset reuse
- **Defensibility** — vs being copied by other orgs
- **Safeguarding** — bajo riesgo to children = high score
