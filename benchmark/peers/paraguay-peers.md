# Paraguayan Peers (8 — direct context)

> These are the 8 PY orgs most directly comparable to or relevant for SOS Paraguay. All are based in Asunción or have PY operations.

## 1. Fundación Paraguaya (Martin Burt)

- **Founded**: 1985 (under Stroessner — founding was an act of courage)
- **Model**: Social enterprise + micro-finance pioneer
- **Why relevant**: Gold-standard PY NGO. SOS could partner on transition-to-adulthood programs for Aldea youth.
- **What to learn**: Self-funded social enterprise model. Could Aldea run a catering / bakery / training center to fund itself?

## 2. CIRD (Centro de Información y Recursos para el Desarrollo)

- **Revenue**: $20.8M (Aug 2024 zoominfo)
- **Tech**: WhatsApp + RVE — 25K+ messages to parents for child immunization
- **Why relevant**: WhatsApp at scale is **exactly** the model SOS PY could replicate for family strengthening in Proyecto Ojoykére.
- **What to learn**: Replicate their WhatsApp + RVE integration. Partner on outreach.

## 3. UNICEF Paraguay

- **Model**: UN agency country office
- **Why relevant**: Government/multilateral channel. Potential grant recipient.
- **What to learn**: Apply for UNICEF PY grants.

## 4. Fundación Solidaridad

- **Headcount**: 60 employees
- **Model**: Volunteer options (presencial missions for disability)
- **Why relevant**: Strong on volunteer pipeline.
- **What to learn**: Volunteer tiering + recognition system.

## 5. Fundación Cimientos

- **Model**: Education equity (secondary school persistence)
- **Outcomes**: 83% graduates continue studying/working
- **Why relevant**: Direct peer (child education)
- **What to learn**: Outcome measurement framework

## 6. Fundación Alda

- **Founded**: 2003 (23 years)
- **Mission**: Education + quality of life for children
- **Address**: Teniente Ramírez Torres 242, Asunción
- **Why relevant**: Long-running PY child-focused org
- **What to learn**: 23-year sustainability story; possible partner for Ojoykére

## 7. TECHO Paraguay

- **Model**: Volunteer-led construction of emergency housing
- **PY**: 10,000+ beneficiaries from 2024 Colecta Nacional (campaign exceeded goal)
- **Why relevant**: Active fundraising; transparency leadership
- **What to learn**: 
 - Public rendición de cuentas page (template)
 - Annual Colecta Nacional — replicate the "single campaign that exceeds goal" pattern

## 8. Fundación La Caixa Proinfancia

- **Active in**: Spain + LatAm
- **Program**: Childhood poverty prevention
- **Why relevant**: International foundation funding model
- **What to learn**: Integrated service bundles (education + health + leisure)
