# International Peers (9)

## 1. SOS Children's Villages INTERNATIONAL (the umbrella)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| 2024 combined revenue | **€1.72 billion** (~USD 1.86B) |
| Donations by individuals | €782M (45%) |
| Government subsidies | €608M (35%) |
| Total expenditure | €1.65B |
| % to programs | 76% |
| % to fund development | 13% |
| % to overhead | 11% |
| Audited? | Yes, annually by independent auditors |
| Public executive salaries | Disclosed (President/CEO/CPO/COO/Special Rep = €180k–220k gross in 2019) |
| Transparency URL | https://www.sos-childrensvillages.org/financial-data |

**Key takeaway for SOS Paraguay**: the umbrella *has* public financial data — but Paraguay does NOT publish its own. They could/should mirror this transparency.

## 2. SOS Children's Villages ESPAÑA (2024)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Children/youth served | **59,317** (10% YoY growth) |
| Families served | 8,471 |
| Spain domestic programs | 29,759 beneficiaries |
| International programs | 29,558 beneficiaries |
| Spain programs | 32 Day Centers, 9 Family Programs, 5 Early Childhood Education Centers |
| President | Pedro Puig (public profile, advocates on child poverty) |

**Comparison vs Paraguay**:
- Spain serves **60× more beneficiaries** than Paraguay (59,317 vs ~1,000)
- Spain has a documented **Memory 2024** annual report download — Paraguay doesn't
- Spain president actively appears in press — Paraguay's director is invisible in public
- **Most directly comparable benchmark** since both are national SOS members

## 3. Compassion International

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| HQ | Colorado Springs, USA |
| Model | **Sponsorship** at $43/month, tied to specific child |
| Letters processed | ~9M/year (2019) |
| Sponsor portal | Centralized communication center (login) |
| Email list | 950,000+ subscribers |
| Financial transparency | Yes — Charity Navigator rated |

**Key insight**: This is the *exact* product Aldea SOS Paraguay is missing. Compassion is a sponsorship machine — letters, photos, portal login, dashboards.

## 4. Plan International (formerly Foster Parents Plan)

- Origin: 1937, "Foster Parents Plan for War Children"
- Model: Child sponsorship + programs
- Donor loyalty team with dedicated 800 number + donor portal
- Active sponsor-visit program (in-person visits to sponsored child)
- Tech: online child-selection portal

**Insight**: Plan has a donor-visit experience. Paraguay's tourism + diaspora market could power a "visit your sponsored child" program — but this needs safeguarding.

## 5. ChildFund International

- Public annual reports + 990s downloadable
- "International offices" model (autonomous + federation)
- Strong digital transparency
- "We don't use your gift for administrative costs" framing

## 6. World Vision

- 35.2M people assisted globally (2024) — including 19M children
- 87 natural disasters responded to
- Massive scale + active digital storytelling

## 7. War Child Alliance

- 2024 revenue exceeded budget by 12%
- 100% growth in children reached (after merger)
- Multi-entity federation (like SOS) — annual report as a federation

## 8. Save the Children

- Operates in 120+ countries
- 29 member countries
- Public financials + Charity Navigator rating
- Strong digital storytelling + emergency campaigns

## 9. Terre des Hommes

- 82% donations to projects in 2025
- Innovation focus: environmental rights, online exploitation, forced labor
- Annual reports downloadable
