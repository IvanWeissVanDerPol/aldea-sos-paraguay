## Brechas de capacidades (top 8)

1. **No donación online** — every single peer has it. Este es the #1 unlock.
2. **No memoria anual download** — even TECHO Paraguay publishes one.
3. **No portal de apadrinamiento** — even SOS Spain has it vía the federation.
4. **No charity rating** — Charity Navigator, Give.org, etc.
5. **No Spanish/English version** — even if limited to "donate from abroad" page.
6. **No TikTok** — every major peer is there.
7. **No Google Ad Grants** — free $10k/mo disponible to any qualified nonprofit.
8. **No volunteer portal** — separate from "trabaja con nosotros".
