## Qué they do that SOS PY should copy

### WhatsApp + RVE for child immunization
CIRD has built a **WhatsApp-integrated vaccination reminder system** that:
- Pulls vaccination status from the national RVE (Registro de Vacunación Electrónica)
- Sends WhatsApp messages to parents when vaccines are due
- Has sent **25,000+ messages** and reached **8,131 children** as of June 2026
- Publicly reported their impact metrics

Source: cird.org.py news June 2026.
