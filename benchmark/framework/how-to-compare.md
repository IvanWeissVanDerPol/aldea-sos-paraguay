# How to Compare Child-Welfare / Child-Sponsorship NGOs

A rubric for evaluating peer organizations. Use this to assess any new peer before adding to the catalog.

## Dimensions to score (1-5 each)

### 1. Mission clarity
- 1 = vague mission
- 3 = mission clear, programs slightly off-mission
- 5 = one-sentence mission, all programs aligned

### 2. Service model
- Which model? (residential care, sponsorship, prevention, foster, kinship, education, health, emergency, rights, mixed)
- Reach (per country / per program)
- Scale (children served/year)

### 3. Digital infrastructure
- Website (live, dead, what stack)
- Donation gateway (1, multiple, none)
- CRM (yes, no)
- Email list (size, tool)
- Social platforms (IG, FB, TikTok, X, YouTube)
- Volunteer application flow
- Multilingual?

### 4. Revenue model
- One-time donations
- Recurring giving (padrinos, sponsorships)
- Corporate partnerships (count)
- Government contracts
- Grants (international, local)
- Earned revenue (grooming, training, events)
- Crypto / Web3?

### 5. Brand & visibility
- Founder-anchored vs org-anchored
- Trademark registered?
- Spokesperson in press?
- Storytelling quality

### 6. Compliance
- NGO registered?
- Tax-exempt?
- Audited financials public?
- Annual report public?
- Safeguarding policy public?

### 7. Engagement (proxy for traction)
- IG followers
- FB likes
- TikTok followers
- Email list size
- Recurring donor count
- Volunteer count

### 8. Crisis preparedness
- Published safeguarding policy?
- Incident response protocol?
- Comms playbook?

## Scoring convention

For each peer:
- Total score = sum of dimension scores (max 40)
- Category strength = average of relevant dimensions

## Caveats

- Self-reported metrics are inflated — cross-check with audited reports where possible
- Engagement numbers ≠ impact
- A "small" org can outperform a "large" org on per-beneficiary cost
