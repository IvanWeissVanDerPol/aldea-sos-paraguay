## Strategic implications

**The opportunity set is large enough that even converting 5-7 of them would 50-100% revenue impact.** The risks are real but mostly mitigated by a strong existing brand, quick public response capability, and the Federation's own actions.

**The biggest single action**: ship an online donation gateway + transparency microsite within 90 days. Both address multiple weaknesses (W1, W2, W3, W6) and unlock multiple opportunities (O1, O2, O3, O4, O7).
