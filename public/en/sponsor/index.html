How to sponsor

Demo sign-up. The real commitment is made on the official site.

Demo — this is not the official channel. The organization runs its own sponsorship programme, including identity verification, allocation of sponsored children, and follow-up. To begin the real process, visit aldeasinfantiles.org.py/como-ayudar/dona. The form below simulates the sign-up but does not reach the organization.

How it works

  1. Choose your monthly amount. Historical reference minimum: Gs. 50,000.
  2. Complete your sign-up. Personal details + payment method.
  3. The organization assigns you a sponsored child. It verifies your identity and ability to pay, then connects you with a child or young person.
  4. Keep the commitment. You receive letters and periodic updates.
  5. You can cancel at any time. No penalty.

What your sponsored child receives

  • Balanced daily meals.
  • Housing in a family home.
  • Quality education.
  • Preventive and curative medical care.
  • Ongoing psychosocial support.
  • A stable figure: the SOS mother.
