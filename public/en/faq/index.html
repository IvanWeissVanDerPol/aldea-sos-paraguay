FAQ

About this demo

What is this site?

A pro-bono redesign proposal of the Aldeas Infantiles SOS Paraguay website. Built by independent volunteers. Not the official site.

Why does it look different from aldeasinfantiles.org.py?

It is a different site. The organization has not adopted, endorsed, or modified this redesign.

Can I donate through this site?

No. Donation forms here are simulations only. For real donations, visit aldeasinfantiles.org.py.

Is my data collected?

No. Form inputs stay in your browser. The site does not transmit data to any server beyond standard static-asset requests.

About the organization

Where does the organization operate?

5 villages across Paraguay: Asunción, Luque, San Ignacio, Hohenau, Belén. Plus the Ojoykére prevention program (2024).

How many children does it serve?

The organization publicly states more than 1,000 children and young people per year across Paraguay.

How can I contact them?

Visit aldeasinfantiles.org.py/web/contactanos.
