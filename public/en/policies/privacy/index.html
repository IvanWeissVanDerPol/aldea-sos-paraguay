Privacy policy (demo)

Applies only to this demo. Not the organization's privacy notice.

What this demo does with your data

This demo at aldea-sos.paragu-ai.com does not collect, transmit, or store personal data on any server. The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages.

What stays in your browser

  • Theme preference (light/dark mode).
  • Mock login session for the donor portal demo (email + flag indicating "logged in").
  • Optional form values — if you fill a demo form, the form handler returns a fake success receipt ID locally. Nothing is sent anywhere.

What is NOT collected

  • No analytics, no cookies, no trackers, no third-party scripts.
  • No IP address logging, no fingerprinting.
  • No emails are sent to you, the organization, or anyone else.
  • No payments are processed — all donation/sponsor forms are simulations.

Cookies

This demo uses localStorage instead of cookies. localStorage is not transmitted with every HTTP request and is not accessible to other sites on different domains. It is cleared when you clear your browser data.

Children's privacy

The organization serves children and young people in vulnerable situations. This demo:

  • Does not knowingly collect data from minors.
  • Does not display identifying information about children in care.
  • All "stories" in the Stories section are explicitly marked as illustrative, fictional, and not identifying real individuals.

Third-party services

This demo embeds no third-party scripts, fonts, or trackers. The site loads only:

  • Its own CSS, JavaScript, and assets.
  • External links to the official site aldeasinfantiles.org.py for any real action.

Your rights

Because nothing leaves your browser, there is nothing to delete from a server. If you filled a demo form, your data exists only in your browser's localStorage. Clear it via your browser settings or by running localStorage.clear() in the console.

Contact

For questions about this demo: open an issue at the GitHub repository.

For questions about the organization's data practices: visit aldeasinfantiles.org.py.
