Corporate alliances

Annual partnerships, campaigns with retail allies, and impact reports for your CSR team.

Demo — this is not the official channel. The organization has a dedicated person for corporate alliances. To start a real conversation, write directly to aldeasinfantiles.org.py/como-ayudar/aliados-corporativos. The form below is a demo: it simulates a submission without routing to anyone.

How alliances work

The organization maintains three main modes of corporate alliance:

1. Annual partnership

An agreed corporate donation commitment over one year, with amount and frequency negotiated. You receive an annual impact report and the option to involve your team in volunteer activities.

2. Retail campaign

Your brand joins a specific campaign — round-up at checkout (e.g. "Turn your change into smiles"), a percentage donation on a product sale, etc. The organization runs the mechanics with your marketing team.

3. Programme partnership

Your company funds a specific organization programme — a village, a prevention programme, an education scholarship. We credit your brand in the programme reports.

4. Corporate volunteering

Your team contributes professional time — design, translation, mentorship, photography. The organization connects your team with the relevant area.

What the organization offers

  • Annual impact report with verifiable figures.
  • Tax-deductible donation certificate.
  • Joint visibility on social media and communications (to be coordinated).
  • Team access to answer technical questions about the programme.

What the organization does not offer

To preserve editorial and operational independence:

  • It does not issue compliance certifications for your CSR report.
  • It does not lobby on public policies in your company's name.
  • It does not share personal data of children, young people, or families.
