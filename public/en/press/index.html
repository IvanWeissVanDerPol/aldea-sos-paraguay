Context in one paragraph

Aldeas Infantiles SOS Paraguay (RUC 80016122) is a Paraguayan non-profit association and an autonomous member of SOS Children's Villages International (136 countries, founded in 1949). The organization operates 5 villages in Paraguay (Asunción, Luque, San Ignacio, Hohenau, Belén) plus the Ojoykére community prevention program (launched 2024). The organization publicly states it serves more than 1,000 children and young people per year across Paraguay.

Red lines for your piece

Things you should not assert without direct confirmation from the organization:

  • The composition of the board of directors (not published online).
  • Annual budget breakdown by program or village.
  • The amounts transferred annually from the global federation to Paraguay.
  • The number of paid staff and active volunteers.
  • Current minimum sponsorship or donation amounts.
  • The identification of any specific child or family receiving care.
