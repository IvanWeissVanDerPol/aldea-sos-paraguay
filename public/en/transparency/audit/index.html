External audit

Financial statements audited annually by independent firms.

Demo — this is not the official site. The real data is in the public information section of aldeasinfantiles.org.py/informacion-publica. This page reflects the type of content and format that would appear here.

The organization publishes its annual financial statements audited by independent firms. The audit covers:

  • Income statements and balance sheets.
  • Procedures for collecting and disbursing donations.
  • Compliance with tax and regulatory obligations.
  • Administrative procedures.

2024 audit

The 2024 external audit is available in PDF format. It covers the fiscal year ending 31 December 2024, with an unqualified opinion.
