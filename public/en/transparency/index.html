Transparency

What the organization regularly publishes so that anyone can verify how it operates.

Transparency is a central part of the organization's work. This section reproduces the type of information they publish regularly:

External audit

Financial statements audited annually by independent national and international firms. The most recent is the 2024 audit.

Financial information

Corporate governance

Governance structure of the organization in Paraguay: Board of Directors, National Safeguarding Committee, and coordination with SOS Children's Villages International.

Child safeguarding

The organization publishes the existence of its National Child, Youth and Adult Safeguarding Committee, with a zero-tolerance policy. The detailed policy is not public online and must be requested directly from the organization.
