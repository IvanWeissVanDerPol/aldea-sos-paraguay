Corporate governance

Governance structure and coordination with the international network.

Demo — this is not the official site. The real data is in the public information section of aldeasinfantiles.org.py/informacion-publica. This page reflects the type of content and format that would appear here.

The organization operates as an autonomous legal entity under Paraguayan law. The governance structure comprises:

Board of Directors

The Board is the organization's governance body. It sets strategic priorities, supervises the National Director, and approves the financial statements.

National Safeguarding Committee

Independent oversight of child, youth, and adult safeguarding. It is one of the three formal bodies of the SOS Children's Villages system for child protection.

Coordination with SOS Children's Villages International

The organization in Paraguay is an autonomous member of SOS Children's Villages International, present in 136 countries. The relationship includes:

  • Access to global care and safeguarding standards.
  • Additional external auditing.
  • Technical assistance and training.
  • Annual financial transfers (specific amount not public).

What is not public

The named composition of the Board, the National Director's name, and the names of specific senior staff are not public online at the close of this audit. The organization can share that information on direct request.
