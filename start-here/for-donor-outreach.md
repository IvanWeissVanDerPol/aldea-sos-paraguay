# For donor / corporate outreach

**Audience**: Whoever is using this research to ask for money or in-kind support from third parties (Tigo, Itaú, Ueno, Areté, Personal, foundations, etc.).

## The framing per donor type

### Corporate allies (Tigo, Itaú, Ueno, Areté, Personal, Tupi...)
**What they want**: brand association, employee engagement, ESG metrics, customer loyalty.
**What we offer**: 
- "Tu Empresa por la Niñez" — corporate volunteer day package at the Aldeas (priced Gs. 5-15M per day)
- "Sponsor an Aldea" — recurring CSR sponsorship at Gs. 1-5M/month
- "Regalamos Sonrisas" expansion — replicate the TUPI 6-year partnership model
- Skills-based volunteering — pro bono legal/tech/marketing days

→ Use [`07-outreach/email/cold-outreach-corporate.md`](../07-outreach/email/cold-outreach-corporate.md) as the template.

### Foundations & tech-for-good (TechSoup, Google, Microsoft, Salesforce, AWS)
**What they want**: case studies, logos, impact metrics.
**What we offer**:
- They get to be featured as the "tech partner" of a 55-year-old brand
- They get measurable impact (donation volume, recurring donor count)
- They get a story for their own quarterly impact reports

→ Use [`07-outreach/email/cold-outreach-tech-partner.md`](../07-outreach/email/cold-outreach-tech-partner.md).

### Foundations (international, family foundations)
**What they want**: outcomes, sustainability, evidence.
**What we offer**:
- A 90-day pilot with measurable KPIs
- A copublished transparency report
- Logo on the new transparency microsite

→ Use [`07-outreach/email/cold-outreach-foundation.md`](../07-outreach/email/cold-outreach-foundation.md).

### Donor-Advised Funds (Fidelity Charitable, Schwab, NPT, Daffy)
**What they want**: to fund international NGOs from US tax-advantaged accounts.
**What we offer**:
- Register as eligible grantee on the DAF platform
- USD-denominated donation via Stripe for tax receipts
- Quarterly impact reports emailed to DAF donors

→ Use [`07-outreach/email/cold-outreach-donor-daf.md`](../07-outreach/email/cold-outreach-donor-daf.md).

## Numbers to cite (always with sources)

- **Children served**: 1,000+ (aldeasinfantiles.org.py)
- **Aldeas**: 5 (Asunción, Luque, San Ignacio, Hohenau, Belén)
- **Founded**: 1970 in PY (55 years)
- **Federation**: 136 countries, 130k+ children served globally
- **2024 SOS Intl combined revenue**: €1.72B; 76% to programs ([source](https://www.sos-childrensvillages.org/financial-data))
- **Teletón Comilona 2026**: Gs. 1,391,035,000 (USD ~190k) raised in a single event ([source](https://a24.com.py/teleton-logra-recaudacion-historica-de-g-1-391-millones-en-la-comilona-2026/))
- **PY e-commerce market**: USD ~1.2B; digital payment adoption 55.5%
- **Peer benchmark**: 99% of 939 surveyed peer orgs accept online donations

## Numbers to NEVER cite without checking

- Total PY revenue (unknown — would need BCP/SET data)
- Donor count (unknown)
- Cost per beneficiary (unknown — depends on allocation)
- Any "we'll raise X" projection (those are estimates, not commitments)

## Things to never promise in cold outreach

- "We'll raise X in Y months" — we don't run the org
- Specific tech delivery dates — they're proposals
- Specific donor introductions — without permission
- "No scandal risk" — there's always risk; we mitigate, don't deny
