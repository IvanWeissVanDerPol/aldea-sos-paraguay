## The client

**Aldeas Infantiles SOS Paraguay** — a 55-year-old Paraguayan NGO, autonomous member of SOS Children's Villages International (136 countries). Five aldeas in Paraguay serving **1,000+ children** who have lost or are at risk of losing parental care.

- Aldea Asunción (Zeballos Cué neighborhood)
- Aldea Luque
- Aldea San Ignacio (Misiones)
- Aldea Hohenau (Itapúa)
- Aldea Belén (Concepción)

Plus a 2024 prevention program called **Proyecto Ojoykére** in vulnerable neighborhoods.

HQ: Cerro Corá 1155 c/ Brasil, Asunción. RUC 80016122.

---

## The gap

They are **operationally solid but digitally behind**. Specific gaps:

- ❌ No online donation page works (form only — no payment gateway)
- ❌ `/transparencia` URL returns 404 (transparency page is broken)
- ❌ No public annual report
- ❌ No sponsor portal (Compassion/Plan-style)
- ❌ No TikTok (every major peer has one)
- ❌ No Google Ad Grants activated ($10k/mo free for nonprofits)
- ❌ No public board composition / governance info
- ❌ No safeguarding policy publicly disclosed

**Comparison**: 99% of peer child-welfare orgs (939 surveyed) accept online donations. Aldea PY does not.

**Plus** the 2025 Gmeiner scandal — the SOS founder was accused of historical sexual abuse (Austria, 1950s-80s); the Austrian branch was suspended Oct 23, 2025. Paraguay is autonomous and not implicated, but the brand is shaken globally and donor trust is fragile.

6. **Tech-for-nonprofit SaaS stack**: Salesforce NPSP (free CRM), Microsoft Philanthropies (free Office/cloud), AWS Imagine (free credits), TechSoup membership
7. **Safeguarding policy** — published, with quarterly transparency reports
